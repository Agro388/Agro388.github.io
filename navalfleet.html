Protogen Navy

Fleet Naming Convention

Protogen fleets are named in a structured format: [Role Prefix] – [Celestial Designation] – [Numeric Code]

  • EX → Exploration Fleet
  • DF → Defense Fleet
  • ST → Strategic Fleet
  • GA → Grand Armada

Example: DF–Astra–03 (Defense Fleet, Astra Division, Third Active Fleet)

Fleet Flags

Each fleet has a distinct flag with the Omega symbol and stars representing fleet size and rank.

Peace flag for scientific and exploration missions.

War flag for defense and strategic fleets.

Naval ensign for ships; includes stars for fleet size.

Ship Classes

Protogen vessels are classified based on role and symbolic naming.

  • Scout Class: Orion, Lyra, Vela, Cygnus
  • Frigate / Patrol Class: Ferrum, Titanium, Argent, Cobalt
  • Destroyer Class: Sovereign, Unbound, Ascendant, Vanguard
  • Science Vessel Class: Curiosity, Horizon, Parallax, Archive
  • Flagship Class: Reclamation, Omega Prime, Zenith, Genesis

Grand Admiral Banner

The Grand Admiral’s banner is reserved for command ships and HQ. It represents the highest authority in the Protogen Navy.

Red Omega at the center, double bars, side stars for fleet rank, center star for commander authority.

Fleet History Notes

Stars on fleet flags also record history: each star represents a liberated system or fleet achievement. Removing a star marks a fleet decommissioned or lost in battle.
