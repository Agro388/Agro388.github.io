Experiment RD-000 — “Origin”

Objective: Recreate the Empire’s first successful enhancement protocol, the genesis of all subsequent combat and augmentation programs.

Status: Failed

Classification: Tier-V “Historical Artifact”

Protocol Summary: RD-000 was the Empire’s initial attempt to engineer a super-soldier through chemical, genetic, and cybernetic augmentation. The procedure combined early cybernetic implants, neural interface nodes, and accelerated growth serum to produce highly adaptive combat subjects.

Outcome:

  • A handful of first-generation subjects exhibited extraordinary reflexes, enhanced musculature, and rapid learning capabilities.
  • Containment issues arose as subjects’ behavior became unpredictable, with some displaying early signs of independent thought and territorial aggression.
  • Attempts to recreate the protocol from surviving documentation failed due to corrupted experimental data and lost neural interface schematics.

Impact on Future Programs:

  • RD-000 directly inspired the QV-099 “Sentinel” line, laying the foundation for Protogen development.
  • Psychological and physiological instability observed in RD-000 informed stricter behavioral compliance protocols for all later experiments.
  • A shadow file of RD-000 remains archived under secure protocols, occasionally referenced for experimental theory, but any attempt at reconstruction is forbidden.

Notes: Subjects’ unique tissue responses and partially autonomous neural patterns hint at potential proto-sentience, though this remains classified and unconfirmed. RD-000 is considered the Empire’s forbidden origin, a blueprint that could theoretically surpass current augmentation tech if fully replicated.

Disclaimer: All attempts to replicate or retrieve data are restricted to Tier-V authorized personnel only. Unauthorized interaction may result in subject exposure and containment breaches.
